DNA transcription is the first step of gene expression where a particular segment of DNA is copied into RNA by the enzyme RNA polymerase. During this molecular process, the DNA sequence is read by RNA polymerase, which produces a complementary, antiparallel RNA strand called a primary transcript. This messenger RNA molecule is then transported out of the cell nucleus into the cytoplasm. In the cytoplasm, ribosomes bind to the messenger RNA and initiate the process of translation. Translation is the step where the genetic code carried by mRNA is decoded to produce a specific sequence of amino acids in a polypeptide chain. The newly synthesized polypeptide chain then folds into a functional protein, which carries out essential duties within the cell.
